# Brag Plan: RepairVerse

## What is this app?
A repair simulator in the browser: you get a 3D laptop that overheats, inspect its parts, make a technician's calls, and every choice is scored and explained.

## The angle
"Most repair learning is watching. Here you make the calls." The film watches a learner fix a real fault in about 20 seconds of the real app. A ticket says the laptop shuts down. The learner clicks into the 3D laptop, finds dust, picks the safe fix, and the CPU drops from 96°C to 61°C. The proof is the product's own screens, with no mockups.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The real laptop screen inside the app reads "96 °C · Throttling", punched in close. Beside it, on the app's pale service-manual ground, is the line from the ticket: "Shuts down after 15 minutes."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The cursor clicks the numbered cooling-fan hotspot on the 3D laptop. The camera swings in and the clue panel fills: "Fan spins but sounds strained. A gray layer of dust coats the blades."
- The cursor picks "Power down the laptop and inspect and clean the cooling system safely". The card turns green, and the readout flips to "CPU after cleaning 61°C".
- Mission complete: 100/100.

## Outro / punchline
The RepairVerse mark is drawn line by line, then the cyan callout dot lands. Below it sit the real CTA "Start repair mission" and the line "Free, no account, about 6 minutes."

## User flow worth showing
Ticket (overheating laptop, 96°C) → inspect parts in 3D (click hotspot, read the clue) → choose the repair (61°C after) → score 100/100.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a technician's bench film, service-manual calm
- Interpretation: few scenes and confident holds. Mixed-case Geist with nothing shouted. The product does the talking. Cuts are clean and the moves are camera moves on real UI.

## Duration: 23.6s

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11 (114.84 BPM, strong cue at 1.60s)
- Music treatment: starts at 0. Bed at about 0.5, under the clicks. 1.2s fade-out under the end card.
- Music cue guidance: preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`. Strong cues lock the reveal (3.70s), the inspect cut (6.34s), the decision cut (12.65s) and the score cut (17.91s). The other hits use natural timing.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. Music RMS deepens the shadow under the UI window and the CTA button. No waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse and motion-matched
- Audio-coupled moments: a click on each real cursor click (fan hotspot, repair answer), one soft impact on the reveal, and one bell when the logo dot lands
- Restraint rule: no whooshes on every cut and no sound without a cause on screen
